# Earth MRI watch list

USGS Earth Mapping Resources Initiative (started 2019). Money goes to state
geological surveys and to USGS flights (magnetics, radiometrics, lidar,
hyperspectral). Two buckets: **new maps / chemistry / geophysics**, and
**mine-waste inventory + characterization**.

Purpose: pick the **next place to run the full pipeline** (Tasks 1–11),
not a reason to retrain the Washington forest. A new Earth MRI map is a
bbox and a chemistry table. It is not a new model.

This file is a watch, not a complete award ledger. The live catalog is
always ahead of us.

## Who has been funded

Almost every state has had *something*: a USGS flight over their ground,
a state-mapping cooperative, mine-waste inventory money, or a workshop
seat. FY2025 mapping funded **24 states / 25 projects**. FY2026 mapping
is heading toward **~38 states / 39 projects**. Mine waste has been
**~25 states / year**.

## Publications that have actually shipped

Most Earth MRI “products” are **data releases** (airborne grids, GeMS
maps, chemistry tables), not journal papers. State OFRs lag the award
by 2–4 years.

## Next full-pipeline location (this is the point)

Do not retrain. Do score a new box the same way we did Idaho and
California: frozen forest first (Task 12), then DEM + walk list
(Task 11). Only stand up Tasks 1–10 if the box has NURE, named sites,
and a gold-or-placer story. Earth MRI tells us **where the new maps
and waste tables are about to exist**.

A full pipeline wants: NURE sediment, gold MRDS (or a placer district),
a 30 m DEM, and preferably a mine-waste OFR like WA 2026-02. Missing
those → transfer + walk list only.

## Bottom line — keep watching

1. Earth MRI is how we hear about the next study area. It is not how
   we update `/predict`.
2. **Montana transfer is done (AUC 0.34).** A waste OFR on the MBMG /
   USGS shelf upgrades Task 8 on the existing box. It does not retrain.
3. **Idaho 2026** (Mineral Hill, Phosphoria) is the year to decide
   whether to widen the batholith box or open a second Idaho config.
4. **WA Part 2** and Orient / Nighthawk change ranked *sites*, not the
   study area.
5. Recheck the catalog + pubs page after each autumn award list and
   after the FY2026 mine-waste NOFO closes (27 Mar 2026).

An award is not a publication. A publication is a candidate bbox.
